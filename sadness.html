Scripture for Heavy Days

Bible Verses for Sadness

Some days feel heavier than others for no clear reason at all, and the Bible doesn't pretend otherwise. Elijah, one of its boldest prophets, once sat under a tree and asked God to let him die — and God's response wasn't a lecture. It was rest, food, and a quiet voice.

That pattern repeats throughout scripture: sadness is met with presence, not judgment. The Psalms give language to despair that many people struggle to put into words themselves — "Why art thou cast down, O my soul?" — and then answer it, gently, with hope that doesn't require the sadness to disappear first.

If today is a heavy one, let these verses meet you there instead of asking you to feel differently first. The Bible Verse Memory Game on the Verses4Emotions homepage is a small, steady way to keep returning to scripture like this.

Verses to Hold Onto
Psalm 42:11
"Why art thou cast down, O my soul? and why art thou disquieted within me? hope thou in God: for I shall yet praise him."
Psalm 34:17-18
"The righteous cry, and the LORD heareth, and delivereth them out of all their troubles. The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart."
Matthew 11:28
"Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest."
Isaiah 61:3
"To appoint unto them that mourn in Zion, to give unto them beauty for ashes, the oil of joy for mourning, the garment of praise for the spirit of heaviness."
Psalm 40:1-2
"I waited patiently for the LORD; and he inclined unto me, and heard my cry. He brought me up also out of an horrible pit... and set my feet upon a rock."
Lamentations 3:22-23
"It is of the LORD's mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. They are new every morning."
