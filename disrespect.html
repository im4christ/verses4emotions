Scripture for the Overlooked

Bible Verses About Disrespect

Being disrespected — dismissed, talked over, treated as less than you are — stings in a specific way. Scripture doesn't tell you your feelings are wrong. It reminds you that your worth was never up for someone else's vote in the first place.

David, mocked and belittled more than once, didn't respond by shrinking his sense of self — he brought the situation to God rather than letting someone else's disrespect define him.

If you've been made to feel small today, let these verses remind you who actually gets the final word on your worth. Try the Bible Verse Memory Game on the Verses4Emotions homepage.

Verses to Hold Onto
Psalm 139:14
"I am fearfully and wonderfully made: marvellous are thy works."
Romans 12:19
"Vengeance is mine; I will repay, saith the Lord."
1 Peter 3:9
"Not rendering evil for evil, or railing for railing: but contrariwise blessing."
Proverbs 12:16
"A fool's wrath is presently known: but a prudent man covereth shame."
Psalm 138:6
"Though the LORD be high, yet hath he respect unto the lowly."
