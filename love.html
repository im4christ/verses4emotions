Scripture on Love

Bible Verses About Love

1 Corinthians 13 has become almost synonymous with weddings, but it was written first as a description of what love actually looks like in practice — patient, kind, not easily provoked. Not a feeling, but a set of actions.

That definition matters because it grounds love in something more durable than emotion. Scripture repeatedly ties God's love for people to permanence — "I have loved thee with an everlasting love" — a standard human love is invited to reflect, however imperfectly.

Whether you're thinking about love given or love received today, let these verses shape that. Explore more through the Bible Verse Memory Game on the Verses4Emotions homepage.
