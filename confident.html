Scripture for the Bold Heart

Bible Verses for Confidence

Biblical confidence isn't self-generated — it's consistently rooted in something outside the person holding it. "I can do all things through Christ" is a statement of dependence, not self-sufficiency, which is a very different foundation than most confidence advice offers.

That distinction matters on the days confidence feels hard to find on your own. Scripture doesn't ask you to manufacture certainty — it points to a source of it that doesn't run out.

Whatever you're stepping into today, let these verses steady you. Try the Bible Verse Memory Game on the Verses4Emotions homepage to help them stay close.

Verses to Hold Onto
Philippians 4:13
"I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me."
Hebrews 4:16
"Let us therefore come boldly unto the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy."
Proverbs 3:26
"For the LORD shall be thy confidence."
2 Timothy 1:7
"God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind."
Joshua 1:9
"Be strong and of a good courage; be not afraid, neither be thou dismayed: for the LORD thy God is with thee."
